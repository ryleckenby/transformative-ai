# Engagement Charter (Template)

One page, agreed before the engagement starts, signed by the sponsor and the FDE/enablement lead. If it doesn't fit on a page, the engagement isn't scoped yet. Everything in `[brackets]` gets filled in; everything else is the discipline.

---

## Engagement: [name, e.g. "Support triage, Team X"]

**Date:** [start] → [hard end of timebox]
**Status:** Draft / Signed / Revised after discovery ([date])

### The workflow

[2 to 3 sentences: the workflow as currently understood. What comes in, what goes out, who does it, how often. This section gets rewritten after discovery; note the revision date above.]

### The target

| | Baseline (measured [dates]) | Target | Measured how |
|---|---|---|---|
| [primary metric, e.g. median cycle time] | [value] | [value] | [instrument/source] |
| [paired quality metric, mandatory] | [value] | [no worse than / better than] | [instrument/source] |

*Rule: the engagement does not start until every row is at least "in flight" with a date.*

### The timebox and the verdict

- Discovery: [dates] → revised charter
- Build: [dates] → thin path live with pilot group
- Prove: [dates] → **verdict meeting on [date]: harden or kill.** The verdict is made against the target table above, by [sponsor], in writing. A kill is a legitimate outcome and will be documented, not spun.

### The end state (written now, on purpose)

When this engagement ends, [champion/owner name] owns the workflow: they can operate, monitor, and adjust it without the FDE. Handoff requires [owner] running it solo for two weeks pre-exit, a completed [handoff checklist](handoff-checklist.md), and a scheduled [30/90]-day health check. The FDE's final deliverable is a field report to [platform team/backlog].
