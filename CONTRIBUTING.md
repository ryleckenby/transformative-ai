# Contributing

This is a knowledge project: the artifacts are arguments, models, and templates, and they improve the way arguments do, through challenge, evidence, and revision.

## What's welcome

- **Field evidence.** You ran an engagement or an enablement program and something here was wrong, incomplete, or worked differently than described. This is the most valuable contribution type; open an issue or PR with what you saw (suitably anonymized).
- **Counter-arguments.** The repo tries to steelman its own critiques (see [forward-deployment/04](forward-deployment/04-failure-modes-and-critiques.md)); if a section reads like advertising, that's a bug. File it.
- **Template improvements.** The [playbooks](playbooks/) are meant to be used. If a checklist item earned its place in a real engagement, or a question in the discovery bank never pays off, PR it with a sentence of rationale.
- **Reading list additions** that clear the bar in [READING.md](READING.md): would a practitioner act differently for having read it?
- **Glossary precision.** Terminological fights are welcome in issues; the goal is definitions that make distinctions that matter in practice.

## What's not a fit

- Vendor pitches, tool roundups, or content marketing (see "vendor gravity" in [READING.md](READING.md))
- Capability news. The repo is about the organizational layer, which ages slower.
- Rewrites for style alone; the voice here is opinionated on purpose

## House style

- **Claims over hedges.** Write what you believe with confidence and mark genuine uncertainty explicitly, rather than hedging everything equally.
- **Every claim earns its keep.** Prefer one defended assertion to three unfalsifiable ones. If a claim is testable, say what would falsify it.
- **Practitioner altitude.** Written for someone who has to do this next quarter, not for a keynote.
- **Cross-link.** Concepts are defined once (usually in the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md)) and linked thereafter.
- **Date-stamp the perishable.** Anything about current model capability gets a vintage note; organizational claims can stand unstamped.
- **No em dashes.** Use colons, commas, parentheses, or separate sentences instead.

## Process

1. For substantive changes (new docs, reversed positions), open an issue first to argue the case.
2. For fixes, examples, and template tweaks, PR directly.
3. Content is licensed [CC BY 4.0](LICENSE); by contributing you agree your contribution is too.
